Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Mobility → Cancel. Your access continues until the end of the current paid period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Mobility → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Yes, if you sign in. Open Mobility → tap the person icon in the top right (or Settings → Account) → create an account or sign in. Your streak, completions, and current month sync automatically.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Mobility works fully offline. Sign-in is optional and only needed for cross-device sync.

I'm in pain or unsure about a drill — what should I do?

Stop immediately. Mobility is not medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional, especially with existing injuries or conditions. Tap the "Avoid" notes on each drill for common mistakes to skip.
