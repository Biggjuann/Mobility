Mobility — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Mobility (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is operated by Jon Jung. We respect your privacy and built Mobility to collect as little personal information as possible. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information (optional). If you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password (bcrypt). Without an account, the app works fully offline and we collect nothing.
  • Progress data (synced only if signed in). Completed drills, current month, streak, and basic settings. Stored on your device and, when an account is active, on our servers.
  • Subscription information. When you purchase a subscription, Apple shares an anonymous subscriber identifier and transaction details with RevenueCat (our subscription management provider). We do not see your name, billing address, or payment method.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or biometric data. We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDK.

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate your account and sync progress between devices.
  • To verify and manage your subscription entitlement.
  • To communicate critical service updates (e.g., security issues).

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell or rent your data.

3. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store — handles in-app purchases and billing.
  • RevenueCat — validates subscriptions and provides entitlement status. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Railway — hosts our API and database. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

4. Data storage and security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; we never store or see plaintext passwords.
  • All network traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Progress data is stored in a PostgreSQL database with restricted access.

5. Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of your data by emailing us.
  • Delete — delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us. You can also stop using the app — without an account, no data is ever sent to us.
  • Correct — update your email or password from within the app.

If you live in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR/UK GDPR/CCPA, including the right to object, restrict, and port your data. Requests are honored within 30 days.

6. Children

Mobility is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. International transfers

Our servers are operated in the United States. By using Mobility, you consent to your data being processed in the United States.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will reflect the most recent change. Continued use of Mobility after changes means you accept the updated policy.
